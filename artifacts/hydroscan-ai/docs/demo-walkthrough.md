# HydroScan AI demonstration walkthrough

## Synthetic demonstration

1. Choose the app's demonstration mode and run an analysis.
2. Confirm that the result is labeled synthetic and that no real observation date, CRS, latitude/longitude, or pollution finding is claimed.
3. Inspect the RGB, NDWI, raw-mask, and cleaned-mask layers.
4. Move the NDWI threshold and observe the candidate-water pixel count update.
5. Review the spectral anomaly queue and read each region's score explanation.
6. Open history and download the generated PDF and CSV.
7. Open the methodology page to explain limitations and the absence of historical trends with one date.

The demonstration arrays are generated by the application and are not observations of the Godavari or any other river.

## Uploaded-raster demonstration

1. Prepare four single-band GeoTIFFs for blue/B02, green/B03, red/B04, and NIR/B08.
2. Clip each band to the same study area before upload. Verify that width, height, CRS, and affine transform match.
3. Enter a study-area label and the verified observation date if known.
4. Run the analysis, check valid-pixel coverage and any nodata warnings, and review the generated index and masks.
5. If a CRS and affine transform are available, inspect only the reported transformed locations; otherwise use pixel positions.
6. Download and review the PDF and CSV alongside the original source scene's metadata.

Do not interpret an anomaly priority as pollution without independent evidence. Do not treat a thresholded candidate mask as a verified shoreline.
